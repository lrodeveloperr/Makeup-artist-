# Marketing screenshots

Genuine captures of the game (the release build and its real interface and rendering, not edited), for store
listings and promotion. Each scene comes in two sizes, in the order to use them:

| # | File | Scene |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | `01-lipstick-in-motion.png` | A rich red lipstick going on, mid-stroke, over a soft bronze glam look |
| 2 | `02-highlighter-glow-up.png` | The fan brush sweeping highlighter along her cheekbone: pink shimmer lids, glitter berry gloss, gems and hearts |
| 3 | `03-finished-look.png` | A finished look on the review screen: lavender lids, emerald lenses, glossy pink lips, gold-star gems; the next customer waits by Next |
| 4 | `04-winged-liner.png` | The winged liner mid-flick on gold-shimmer lids, one eye finished |

- **`portrait/`**: phone, 1170 × 2532 (a 390 × 844 screen at 3×)
- **`landscape/`**: desktop, 1920 × 1080

The looks were applied with the game's own tools and colours (some are colours of the day), on four different
customers.
